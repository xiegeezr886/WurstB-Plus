WurstB+ Plus does not depend on the LumaGlass mod at runtime and does not
bundle its jar. The glass look of vanilla controls is a source-level port: the
files below were copied or adapted into this repository.

Ported verbatim (only the resource namespace changed, lumaglass -> wurst):

- assets/wurst/shaders/core/glass.json / .vsh / .fsh
  from assets/lumaglass/shaders/core/glass.json / .vsh / .fsh
  (.vsh/.fsh are byte-identical to upstream; the JSONs differ only in the
  "vertex"/"fragment" namespace)
- assets/wurst/shaders/core/blur.json / .vsh / .fsh
  from assets/lumaglass/shaders/core/blur.json / .vsh / .fsh
  (same: GLSL byte-identical, JSON namespace only)

Adapted into this project's packages and style:

- src/main/java/net/wurstclient/gui/glass/GlassRenderer.java
  from dev/lumaglass/client/GlassRenderer.java plus the capture lifecycle of
  dev/lumaglass/api/client/GlassCanvas.java and GlassFrame.java
- src/main/java/net/wurstclient/gui/glass/GlassStyle.java
  from dev/lumaglass/api/client/GlassStyle.java (fields and validation;
  the glow-colour component is omitted because this port has no glow palette)
- src/main/java/net/wurstclient/gui/glass/GlassButtons.java
  from dev/lumaglass/client/theme/VanillaGlass.java (automatic-theme path,
  including the scrollbar material)
- src/main/java/net/wurstclient/mixin/AbstractButtonMixin.java
  from dev/lumaglass/mixin/client/ButtonMixin.java
- src/main/java/net/wurstclient/mixin/AbstractSliderButtonMixin.java
  from dev/lumaglass/mixin/client/SliderMixin.java
- src/main/java/net/wurstclient/mixin/CheckboxMixin.java
  from dev/lumaglass/mixin/client/CheckboxMixin.java
- src/main/java/net/wurstclient/mixin/SelectionListMixin.java
  from dev/lumaglass/mixin/client/SelectionListMixin.java
  (only its scrollbar branch; the list background / selection-row branches of
  the upstream mixin are not ported)

Default material values (blur 4.8, refraction 24, frost 1, tint 0, highlight 1,
glow off) are taken from LumaGlass GlassConfig defaults; the focused
+0.13 tint / +0.7 highlight adjustment is from VanillaGlass.style().

Deviations from upstream are listed in docs/glass-controls.md.
