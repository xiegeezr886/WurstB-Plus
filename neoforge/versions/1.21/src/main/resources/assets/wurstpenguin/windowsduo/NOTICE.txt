duo_glass.frag adapts win/glass_overlay.py FS_DUO: bottom-edge inverse
projection, Vogel disk sampling, mip LOD, edge coverage and darkening.
DuoGlassMotion adapts its 0.22-per-frame angle smoothing to elapsed time.
The GUI uses premultiplied transparent render targets instead of desktop
screenshots. Maximum tilt 88 degrees, eye distance 2x height, spread 0.42,
darkening 0.001 follow win/config.json. Sampling is reduced from 32 to 12 taps,
with a cached input and 540p effect target. A final fade removes
the hinge at full close. No screen capture, hardware sensor or lock action.
