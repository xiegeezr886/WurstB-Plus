Opai menu presentation and card flip adaptation for WurstB+ Plus / Fabric 1.20.1.
Reference: https://github.com/snmwdd/OpaiBounce
Sources: src/opai/ListScreen.svelte, listScreen.scss, menuMotion.ts, opai.scss.
Upstream: CCBlueX and contributors; Opai / StylesFoundation.
Retained licenses and upstream notices accompany this file.

Adapted to Java/NanoVG: world/server list geometry, row cards, controls and
quint-out card rotation with software affine projection and edge handoff.
World loading, saved servers, pings and confirmation flows use Minecraft's
existing implementations. Account management shares the list and flip timing.
Buttons retain Wurst's translucent rounded style and use Chinese labels.
Existing wallpaper, pale-purple palette, fonts and title layout remain.
At the user's request, the title now fades out for 240ms before the list
starts its flip. Returning lists fold away before the title fades in.
No ViaVersion or proxy backend is added by this visual adaptation.
