OpaiBounce ClickGUI adaptation for WurstB+ Plus v1.5.2

Reference: https://github.com/snmwdd/OpaiBounce
Pinned commit: 6a6bc99 (see reference/clients/v1.5.2-sources.json).

The panel geometry, colors, inline control appearance and motion calculations
are adapted from src/routes/clickgui/{Panel,Module,Description}.svelte,
src/opai/{OpaiSetting,Reveal}.svelte, motion.ts and opai.scss.
The adaptation uses Wurst's existing categories, settings, actions and NanoVG
renderer. It retains the existing MiSans fonts and does not bundle Opai media.

Upstream attribution includes CCBlueX and contributors (LiquidBounce),
Opai / StylesFoundation, Samsara contributors and Jon_awa.
The reference is an unofficial Opai-to-LiquidBounce theme port.
