Original copyright remains with upstream authors and contributors.
The upstream music source also credits Melodify for player/API design.

Ported: queue/transport, JLayer MP3 decoding, Java Sound output, NetEase web API,
weapi encryption, QR login, timed lyrics and lyric lookup.
Adapted: asynchronous cover cache, NanoVG music screen and existing Dynamic Island.
Changes: cancellation generations, bounded downloads, local MP3/LRC, lifecycle cleanup,
fixed pale-purple theme, native Java 17/Fabric APIs and music-aware vanilla soundtrack.
Material Icons font is copied from the upstream asset and keeps its Apache-2.0 license.
MusicScreen/LyricPanel geometry, Material alpha helpers and Spring/Ease motion
are adapted from NeoRaven, including its 840x520 canvas, screen fitting, GUI Scale,
control radii, inline volume slider, lyric wrapping and focus/scroll behavior.
Colors retain the fixed Navigator palette; the category rail is translucent and
the content surface is opaque. Rendering uses native NanoVG rather than Onyx.
Original music recordings and text fonts are not redistributed.
Not included: Android/LWJGL2 game-audio backend, world-space 3D lyrics or Onyx renderer.
Dependencies: JLayer 1.0.1.4 (LGPL-2.1-or-later), ZXing core 3.5.3 (Apache-2.0).
Their original Maven jars are bundled under META-INF/jars; licenses and attribution
are in META-INF/licenses/jlayer and META-INF/licenses/zxing.
Source mapping and verification: docs/MUSIC-NEORAVEN-v1.5.2.md
