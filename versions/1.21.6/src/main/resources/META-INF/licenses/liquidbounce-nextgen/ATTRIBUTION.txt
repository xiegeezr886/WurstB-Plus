The Fabric title menu adapts the circular page structure and staggered fly
transitions of src-theme/src/routes/menu/title/Title.svelte and MainButton.svelte
to native Java/NanoVG. At the user's request, original WurstB+ button sizes,
icons are retained, with Nextgen white translucent fills and hover glow; labels are mainly Chinese and the
clock is omitted. No upstream icons, background, brand logo or fonts are included.
Wurst logo: user-supplied artwork, black backdrop and outer glow removed; antialiased transparent edges retained.
