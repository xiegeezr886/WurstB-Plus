Ported from this client's NeoRavenLoadingShape, m3_loading.frag and Ease:
four radial forms, emphasized cubic-bezier easing, 900 ms morph,
1800 ms rotation and 5% scale pulse. LoadingShape.cs samples the shader's
zero contour for antialiased GDI+ / WPF rendering, shared by the single-EXE
bootstrap and native host. Color remains #B5BEF0.

MusicProgress.vue ports MusicCanvas.wave and MusicTransportMotion:
3.24 px stroke, 40 px wavelength, 28 px/s phase, amplitude ramps,
85/90 ms exponential smoothing and original accent / remaining-track colors.
Adaptations: real download progress, indeterminate probing, reduced motion,
resize handling and lifecycle cleanup. No music assets are included.
